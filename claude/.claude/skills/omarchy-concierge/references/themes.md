# Omarchy Theme Library

Each theme includes color palettes, aesthetic descriptions, and mood matching for personalized recommendations.

> **Current theme:** Run `omarchy-theme-current` to check.
> **Apply theme:** `omarchy-theme-set "<Theme Name>"` (use title case with spaces)

---

## Stock Themes (Built-in)

### Catppuccin

**Aesthetic:** A cozy coffee shop at dusk. Warm pastels softened by espresso-dark backgrounds. Comfortable without being drowsy — like your favorite worn-in sweater.

**Mood:** Warm, inviting, gentle, cozy
**Inspirations:** Artisan cafés, pastel art, comfort food, soft blankets
**Pairs with:** Rainy days, long coding sessions, creative work
**Accent:** Blue `#89b4fa` on deep plum `#1e1e2e`

```toml
accent = "#89b4fa"
foreground = "#cdd6f4"
background = "#1e1e2e"
```
